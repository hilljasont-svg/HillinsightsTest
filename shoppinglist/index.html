The five lunches

Monday–Wednesday · Chicken, broccoli, and rice
Divide cooked chicken into three containers. Add one-fifth of the broccoli to each. Keep rice pouches unopened until lunchtime.
Thursday–Friday · Turkey, sweet potatoes, and broccoli
Divide cooked turkey and sweet potatoes between two containers. Add half of the remaining broccoli to each.

Weights above refer to food before cooking. Portions provide a practical starting point; adjust future batches to appetite. Freeze any extra raw meat purchased in a larger pack.

Prep once

  1. Roast the sweet potatoes. Dice, toss with a little olive oil and seasoning, and roast at 425°F for about 25–35 minutes, turning once, until tender.
  2. Cook the chicken. Season and bake on a separate tray at 425°F, checking around 20 minutes. Time varies with thickness; cook to 165°F in the thickest part.
  3. Brown the turkey. Cook in a skillet, breaking it apart and adding seasoning, until it reaches 165°F.
  4. Cook the broccoli. Steam or microwave following package directions, then divide evenly among five shallow, freezer-safe, microwave-safe containers.
  5. Portion and store promptly. Add the chicken to three containers and turkey with sweet potatoes to two. Label with prep date. Refrigerate the first three lunches and freeze the last two on prep day. Refrigerate or freeze within two hours of cooking, or one hour above 90°F. Keep the refrigerator at 40°F or below.

Microwave and eat

Move a frozen lunch to the refrigerator the night before. Add a splash of water, cover loosely with a microwave-safe lid, and heat. Stir or rearrange halfway through; continue until food reaches 165°F throughout. Microwave times vary with portion size and appliance.

For chicken lunches, heat the rice pouch following its directions and add it after reheating the chicken and broccoli.

Use refrigerated cooked portions within 3–4 days. FoodSafety.gov: storage and reheating guidance.
