Experience

I bring more than 20 years of experience across engineering, enterprise program management, technology transformation, operational systems, and organizational change efforts.

My background spans complex cross-functional environments where technology, operations, leadership priorities, and execution realities intersect in ways that create friction, ambiguity, or stalled progress. That experience shaped an approach grounded less in predefined solutions and more in helping organizations understand how systems, structures, communication patterns, and operational habits influence outcomes over time.

Titles have included engineer, project manager, manager, and consultant, but much of the work required the same underlying practice: understanding how work actually occurred, identifying gaps between intent and execution, and helping people develop practical paths forward. That perspective now grounds my work as a process consultant.
